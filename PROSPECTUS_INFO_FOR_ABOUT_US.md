# Key Information from NIET Prospectus for About Us Section

## 1. Institution History & Identity
- **Former Name**: College of Biomedical Engineering and Applied Sciences (CBEAS)
- **Current Name**: National Institute of Engineering and Technology (NIET)
- **Established**: 2005
- **Unique Status**: 
  - Only college in Nepal providing Biomedical Engineering
  - One of the few colleges in Asia providing higher education in Biomedical Engineering
  - Registered with Nepal Engineering Council
- **Motto**: "Innovating for a Healthier Tomorrow"

## 2. Accreditation & Recognition
- **UGC-QAA Certified Institution**
- **First Achievement**: 
  - First among Purbanchal University colleges to receive QAA Institutional Accreditation Certificate
  - First Engineering College in Kathmandu Valley to receive QAA Institutional Accreditation Certificate from UGC Nepal
- **Award**: Best Private College in Nepal from Ministry of Education (97th National Education Day-2073)
- **Award Amount**: NPR 250,000

## 3. Academic Programs & Seats
- **BE Biomedical Engineering**: 144 Seats
- **B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence**: 48 Seats
- **BE Computer Engineering**: 48 Seats
- **Total**: 240 seats across all programs

## 4. Major Achievements & Milestones

### International Conferences Hosted
- **ICIDN-2023**: International Conference on Infectious Diseases and Nanomedicine (Dec 15-18, 2023, Kathmandu) - Co-hosted
- **Ka SAM-2024**: International Conference on Advanced Materials (Oct 22-24, 2024, Park Village Resort, Hotel Kathmandu) - Co-hosted
- **MMDR 2008 & 2014**: International Conferences on "Medical Materials, Devices and Regenerative Medicine"
  - 2008: Inaugurated by First President of Republic of Nepal, Rt. Honorable Dr. Ram Baran Yadav
  - 2014: Inaugurated by Vice-President of Nepal, Mr. Paramananda Jha
  - Featured distinguished scientists from USA, UK, Australia, Japan, Belgium, China, Singapore, India, and Nepal
- **9th Microsymposium on Applied Sciences (Nano/Bio) 2025**: Successfully held on 29th March 2025 at NIET
  - Students won best poster award
- **2017**: One-day international seminar on "Recent Developments in Biomedical Engineering" in Kathmandu

### Research & Publications
- **Scientific Journal**: "BIOMED-NEPAL" published by the college since 2007
- **International Publications**: 
  - Publications in journals by world-renowned publishers (Wiley, Elsevier, SAGE)
  - Faculty members assigned as guest editors for journals (e.g., SAGE)
  - Publications in journals like PhotoDiagnosis, Photodynamic Therapy, Engineering Reports, Dalton Advanced Research Journal of Engineering in Medicine, International Journal of Creative & Innovative Research In all Studies
- **Student Achievements**: 
  - First prizes in paper and poster presentations at various national and international conferences
